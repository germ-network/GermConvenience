Add `GermCBOR`, a canonical (RFC 8949 §4.2.1) CBOR value-model encoder/decoder for the COSE (RFC 9052) profile — integer/text-keyed maps, arrays, byte/text strings, bool, null; no tags, floats, bignums, or indefinite lengths. Its own library product, Foundation-only, no dependencies. Intended for COSE key/structure encoding, not as a general-purpose CBOR codec.
